Privacy Policy

Steadyfact Media and the Steadyfact Publisher application. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Who we are

Steadyfact Media makes explainer videos and publishes them on its own YouTube channels. Steadyfact Publisher is our internal application. Only Steadyfact Media uses it, and only with Google accounts and YouTube channels that Steadyfact Media owns.

Use of YouTube API Services

Steadyfact Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The application asks Google for these permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos that our team approved to our own channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel name, channel ID and the list of our uploaded videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views and other performance reports for our own videos.

Data we access, use and store

  • We access only the data of channels that Steadyfact Media owns.
  • We use the data only to publish our videos and to learn which videos perform well.
  • We store an OAuth refresh token in an encrypted secret store, so that the application can act for our channel. We store channel and video IDs and summary report numbers for our own records.
  • We do not collect data about viewers or other YouTube users. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

We keep stored data only while we run the channel. We delete stored tokens and report data within 30 days after we stop using the application, or when you ask us to.

You can remove the application's access at any time on the Google security settings page. To ask for deletion of stored data, email us.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we update this page and its date. Questions: steadyfactmedia@gmail.com.
